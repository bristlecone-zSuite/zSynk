Everything up to that point is in the repo: `make release SYNK_CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY="Developer ID Application: …"` signs and verifies; the notarization commands are in docs/XCODE.md "Signing". Until then, CI proves the machinery ad-hoc.
- Phase 4's human half: the done-line says "someone who has never opened a terminal can pair two Macs and sync Obsidian and Keynote." That needs two real Macs and a non-technical pair of hands — the maintainer's validation. The code side (onboarding, pairing, conflict center, iCloud move, signed-build machinery) is complete and tested.

Until then, findings are triaged by running CodeQL locally; see docs/decisions/0002-codeql-triage.md. Local CodeQL attempts on 2026-11-16 failed for tooling reasons (query pack path, then "no Go code found" in the database) and were not pursued.
